Three checkout modes

How a single visit is charged. The app labels these Hourly, ticket, and pack.

Mode 1

Hourly rental

Thuê theo giờ · 시간 요금

Court drop-in whose name includes 코트 and not 레슨. Member picks start and end inside opening hours.

  • 30-minute steps, 60-minute minimum
  • Total = posted hourly rate × stay × quantity
  • Bank transfer QR, or pay at the venue
  • Badminton 옥수 ₩21,600 / hour · pickleball 서울숲 ₩24,000 / hour
Mode 2

Drop-in ticket

Vé lẻ · 분권 / 시간권

A fixed-length visit at a posted drop-in or trial price. No extra time blocks. No pack required.

  • DROP_IN or TRIAL class that is not a court rental
  • Quantity stepper only
  • Pilates 리포머: 50-minute ticket ₩35,000
  • When membership is off, the app never sends the member to buy a pack
Mode 3

Pack checkout

Theo gói · 이용권

A class or lesson paid with a pass. Confirm holds a seat. A session is used only after attend.

  • PACKAGE class, or a court 레슨
  • Pass on file → nothing more is due
  • No matching pass → buy a pack on the same screen
  • Remaining sessions show on You

Three pack kinds

When the member buys a pack, Confirm and the studio Info tab group the shop’s product list. The desk owns the prices on Products. The app only groups them.

Monthly pack · Gói theo tháng · 월 이용권 Session pack · Gói theo buổi · 횟수 이용권 Trainer pack · Gói huấn luyện viên · 강사 이용권 Trial pack · Gói trải nghiệm · 체험
Pack · Period

Monthly pack

Gói tập theo tháng

Product type PERIOD. Calendar validity, often with a weekly frequency.

  • Example: 필라테스 1개월 · 1 month · 2 sessions/week · ₩180,000
  • Example: 키즈 주2회 · monthly at the barre desk
Pack · Count

Session pack

Gói tập theo buổi

Product type COUNT. Remaining sessions on the pass. Default pack when several COUNT products exist.

  • Example: 리포머 8회 · 8 sessions · 6 months · ₩240,000
  • Example: 성인 12회 · 12 sessions · 6 left on file
Pack · Trainer

Trainer pack

Gói huấn luyện viên

A COUNT product whose name matches 레슨, 코치, 강사, 개인, huấn, trainer, or PT. Personal training, not the group class pack.

  • Example: 강사 개인 4회 · 4 sessions · 3 months · ₩280,000
  • Instructors are attached to classes on the desk before slots generate
